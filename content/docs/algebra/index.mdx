Algebra studies linear structure: vectors built by linear combination, maps
that preserve linear combinations and the coordinates that turn both into
lists and matrices. The strand works over a scalar field $F$, usually
$\mathbb R$ for geometric examples and $\mathbb C$ when complex roots or
eigenvalues are needed.

## Chapters

1. [Complex numbers](/algebra/complex-numbers): arithmetic and polar form in
   $\mathbb C$, polynomial roots and roots as modes of discrete and
   continuous systems.
2. [Vector spaces](/algebra/vector-spaces): one setting for $F^n$, polynomial
   spaces $P_n(F)$ and matrix spaces $M_{m\times n}(F)$, developed through
   subspaces, span, independence, bases and coordinates and ending with
   interpolation.
3. [Linear transformations](/algebra/linear-transformations): maps that
   preserve linear combinations, read through their geometry, kernel and
   image and, in finite dimensions, as matrices once bases are chosen.
4. [Eigenvalues](/algebra/eigenvalues): invariant directions and
   diagonalisation, including when a matrix is not diagonalisable over the
   chosen field, applied to matrix powers, [linear ODE
   systems](/algebra/eigenvalues/linear-ode-systems) and [Markov
   chains](/algebra/eigenvalues/markov-systems).

## Links to Calculus

- **Polynomial spaces:** [interpolation](/algebra/vector-spaces/data-fitting)
  and [Taylor models](/calculus/taylor-series/taylor-polynomials) are both
  coordinate problems in a polynomial space.
- **Kernels:** the [kernel](/algebra/linear-transformations/kernel-image-rank)
  of a linear map describes every solution of a homogeneous equation and
  every solution of a forced equation once one particular solution is known.
  This covers matrix equations and [linear
  ODEs](/calculus/ordinary-differential-equations/existence-uniqueness-and-linear-operators)
  alike.
- **Characteristic roots:** [complex characteristic
  roots](/algebra/complex-numbers/stability-of-systems) describe the growth,
  decay and oscillation of recurrences and of the [differential
  equations](/calculus/ordinary-differential-equations/second-order-constant-coefficient-equations)
  in Calculus.
